Process flow

Ready-to-eat beef jerky · Carne Seca Jesus Canales, LLC · Delta, UT

The customer does not cook it. The product contains beef and is sold shelf-stable, without refrigeration.

Process step
Critical control point
The lot does not ship
1. Receive inspected beef Invoice and mark of inspection. Measure the product temperature.
2. Receive salt, spices, and bags The specification matches. No nitrite. No soy. No wheat.
3. Cold storage — not a CCP Product below 41°F. Prerequisite. Probe the meat, not the air.
4. Slice, weigh, marinate, single layer Still below 41°F. Do not stack. Do not dry before the cook.
CCP 1 · COOKING, BEFORE DRYING
Heat lethality
  • Internal 145°F for at least 4 minutes, thickest piece, coldest spot
  • Wet bulb at least 125°F for at least 1 hour
  • Relative humidity at least 27% for at least 1 hour
  • Oven sealed for 1 hour or 50% of the cook, whichever is longer
  • Dampers closed within 30 minutes
  • From 50°F to 130°F: 6 hours or less
CCP 2 · DRYING
Water activity
  • Dry bulb at least 170°F during drying
  • Water activity 0.85 or less, before packing
  • At least six pieces. The highest reading is the result.
  • Moisture-to-protein ratio is not the critical limit.
5. Cool dry, pack, and label Do not rinse. Only if both CCPs passed. Lot code on the bag.
6. Review the records and sign Pre-shipment review under 9 CFR 417.5. Then dry storage.
If a CCP limit is missed, the lot is held and is not sold as ready-to-eat beef jerky.
